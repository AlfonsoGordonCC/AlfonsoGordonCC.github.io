ALFONSO GORDON CABELLO DE LOS COBOS

One of the skills I honed, and that I believe is quite important for my personal and career goals, is 3D modeling. These tools have become essential for my mission mainly because they allow the development of more personalized equipment.

These projects were made by using CAD tools (SolidWorks) or free 3D modeling ones (Autodesk 3D Max).

Desk

  • CAD

This was my final project for the ENGN 1740 (Computer Aided Design and Visualization) course at Brown University. The full desk was built from scratch and no external sources where used, even the bolts and screws were handmade, based on measures from a caliper and a measuring tape.

  • CAD

Tip set

Another asignment from ENGN 1740. This time is a full set of screwdriver tips that were use for a larger group project where we designed a full toolbox. Each tip was first designed independently and then added to the holder.

Stepped part

  • CAD

The assigment in this case was to recreate a piece given by the proffesor on SolidWorks. What I did was to create the drawings and measures for the different views and then use the measurement tool for creating the model.
