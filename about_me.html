My career on Biomedical Engineering has been mainly focused on biomechanics and medical devices. My goal is to help as much people as possible whether it's by developing wearables for daily use, creating applications and experiences that can engage rehabilitation patients or assesing different medical devices.

EDUCATION

Brown University

Biomedical Engineering MSc (2024-2026)

After thinking about the skills acquired during my Bachelor's degree and the ones missing, I decided to pursue a Masters Degree. Being accepted at one of the most prestigious universities worldwide was a complete dream that became true. This master was really interesting because of the variety of courses that were offered. In my case, I decided to complete my education by taking:

  • Principles Exercise Physiology: If I wanted to develop devices meant for everyday uses, I had to strengthen my knowledge on how the human body reacts to the exercise and to the efforts that could be made
  • Biomaterials: Essential for the developing of different prosthetics and medical devices as well as for understanding how materials' science work and how to select the most suitable material for the different projects.
  • Deep Learning: Artificial Intelligence has become a topic of great interest. I wanted to gain more insights not only on the programming but also on how the different types of networks work and the different uses they might have.
  • Topics on Bioelectronics: The design of medical devices require a great understanding of how the different circuits work, the components that are currently used and their effects.
  • Evaluation of Health Information Systems: Developing a device comes toghether with the responsability of knowing how to assess its impact and how to make sure that it helps both patients and clinicians.
  • Neuroengineering: Greater insight on how the brain worked and how neural signals can be recorded and used.
  • Computer Aided Visualization and Design: Strengthening the designing aspects of the devices and practice how to use CAD software.
